## What Wattnet is

Wattnet is developed by the Advanced Computing and e-Science Group at the Institute of Physics of Cantabria (IFCA), a joint center of the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC) and the University of Cantabria, as part of the [GreenDIGIT](https://greendigit-project.eu/) project, funded by the EU's Horizon Europe programme (grant agreement No. 101131207) and the Swiss State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation (SERI).

It provides real-time, historical, and forecasted data on the carbon and water footprint of electricity across Europe, built from sources such as ENTSO-E, Elexon, and EPIAS, and combined through Wattnet's own flow-tracing methodology. As with any research project, Wattnet is under active development, so endpoints, data formats, and access conditions may change as it evolves and as new grid zones or data sources are added.

## Using the dashboard and API

The [dashboard](https://dashboard.wattnet.eu) is open to everyone, free of charge, and requires no registration. Access to the [API](https://api.wattnet.eu/docs) works a little differently: it's granted on request, so if you'd like to integrate Wattnet data into your own tools or research, get in touch through our [contact page](/contact) to request access. Once approved, your API token is issued through the [developer portal](https://api.wattnet.eu/token-request), and from then on the API is yours to use.

There are currently no enforced rate limits on API access, though this may change as usage of the Service grows. If we do introduce limits, they will be documented in the API reference rather than applied without notice. Please don't attempt to circumvent any conditions attached to your access, for example by requesting multiple tokens or rotating IP addresses, since doing so affects the quality of the Service for everyone else relying on it. For the same reason, we may suspend or revoke access that is clearly being misused or that is negatively affecting the Service for other users.

## Requesting and using API access

When you request access, we'd appreciate it if you could give us an accurate idea of how you intend to use the data, since this helps us understand who relies on the Service and plan its future accordingly. Once you have a token, please treat it as you would any other credential:

- Keep it confidential, and avoid sharing or publishing it anywhere it could be picked up by others.
- Let us know promptly if it's ever exposed or compromised, so we can issue you a new one.
- Reach out if your use case changes significantly, so we can make sure your access still fits.

Any information you share with us when requesting access is used only to manage that access and to respond to your enquiry.

## About the data

Carbon and water footprint figures published through Wattnet are estimates, built from models and third-party sources such as ENTSO-E, Elexon, and EPIAS, and they can therefore contain errors, gaps, or delays that are outside our control. We do our best to keep the underlying pipelines accurate and up to date, but we don't guarantee that the data is complete, error-free, or continuously available.

Wattnet is provided as a research tool rather than a certified measurement service, and we are not responsible for decisions or actions taken on the basis of the data it provides. If you're relying on it for something important, such as a business, regulatory, or otherwise consequential decision, please validate the figures independently rather than relying on Wattnet as your sole source.
